# 🏨 Stayora

## ✈️ AI-Powered Travel & Stay Discovery Platform

Stayora is a full-stack travel and accommodation platform designed to simplify the way people discover destinations, plan vacations, find suitable stays, and make bookings.

### 💡 Core Idea

-> Tell us your budget, travel duration, preferences, and travel style — Stayora helps you discover where to go, what to do, and where to stay.

The current platform provides property discovery, search and filtering, wishlist, booking, reviews, host management, image uploads, and online payment functionality.

🚀 The future vision of Stayora is to integrate **AI-powered personalized travel planning**, including destination recommendations, budget optimization, intelligent stay recommendations, and AI-generated itineraries.
